Wellness For Black Women

What is Wellness For Black Women?

Black Women's Wellness is a health movement that helps Black women live long, healthy, and happy lives. It protects their physical and mental health. It also fights against fair treatment gaps in doctors' offices.

Blacks Women's Health

Black women's health is a major area of focus because Black women face higher rates of serious illnesses and lower quality medical care. Widespread racism, daily stress, and medical bias create large gaps in how they are treated.
